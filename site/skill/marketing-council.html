When to reach for it

When the user wants several expert takes on a marketing decision, or asks what a famous marketer would say about it: a simulated board (Seth Godin, David Ogilvy, April Dunford, Byron Sharp, and more), each grounded in their documented frameworks, where they disagree, and a recommendation.

How to use it

The menu has copy-paste setup steps for Claude Code, the Claude app, ChatGPT, Codex, Gemini CLI, GitHub Copilot, Cursor and other agents. You can download it from the menu as a ready-to-use folder, or copy its instructions. Once it’s added, just ask your AI for the task and it uses the skill when it fits.
